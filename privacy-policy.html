Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Who we are

FORM·A dsgn is an architecture and interior design studio working between Kyiv, Ukraine and Bratislava, Slovakia. This website (formadsgn.com) is our studio portfolio. This policy explains what information we collect through the site and how it is used.

What we collect

We do not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or tracking cookies of any kind. The only personal information we collect is what you choose to submit yourself, through the contact form on this site: your name, phone number and email address.

How we use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your inquiry — for example, to get in touch about a potential project. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties, other than the service that delivers the form submission to us (see below).

Form processing

Our contact form is handled by Formspree, a third-party form-delivery service that forwards your submission to our email inbox. Formspree processes this data on our behalf; we encourage you to review Formspree's own privacy policy for details on how they handle data in transit.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking purposes. Any cookies present are limited to what your browser or GitHub Pages hosting uses to serve the site itself, and are not used to identify or profile visitors.

How long we keep it

We keep messages received through the contact form only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for reasonable record-keeping afterwards. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Where your data is processed

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are processed by Formspree. Both are used by studios and businesses worldwide, including within the EU; if you are located in the European Union, this means your data may be processed outside your country in the course of these services being provided.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To do so, just email us directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your data, can be sent to formadsgn.studio@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
